Who we are

Noble Pathfinder Charity Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to empowering vulnerable individuals, such as widows and school children, through compassion-driven initiatives, sustainable support systems and impactful social programs.

Founded on service, integrity and community development, we strive to be a guiding light for those in need, helping them navigate challenges and build better futures. Through partnerships, volunteerism and community engagement, we are committed to creating lasting change.

Every individual deserves dignity, opportunity and hope.

Funding proposal

Noble Pathfinder Charity Foundation seeks funding to expand programmes that improve quality of life for underserved people in our community, focusing on education, medical outreach and humanitarian aid for widows, youth and school children.

The problem

  • Limited access to quality education
  • Youth unemployment and lack of vocational skills
  • Poor access to basic healthcare
  • Food insecurity and poverty

Our objectives

  • Educational support for at least 50 children every year
  • Empower at least 50 widows
  • Promote health awareness and access to basic medical support

Proposed programmes

  • Education initiative: scholarships and school materials
  • Community outreach and welfare: support for widows and disadvantaged school children
  • Health campaigns: free medical outreaches and awareness workshops

Expected impact

  • Higher school enrolment and retention
  • Reduced poverty among widows in target communities
  • Better health awareness and well-being

Funding requirements

  • Programme implementation
  • Community outreach activities
  • Administrative and operational costs

A detailed budget breakdown is available on request.

Monitoring and evaluation

  • Beneficiary data collection
  • Programme reports and impact assessments
  • Regular monitoring and evaluation reviews

Sustainability plan

  • Build strategic partnerships
  • Encourage community participation
  • Develop income-generating initiatives to support programmes

Working with partners

Our Widows' Day and medical outreach were organised in partnership with The Believing Women Organization, Agege, Lagos chapter. We welcome NGOs, businesses, schools and individuals who want to serve alongside us.
